# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Basketball coaches preparing and managing a team, especially while running a game. They need to set up a roster and starting five, manage substitutions and playing time in real time, and review player usage afterward.

## Product Purpose

BenchMaster helps coaches prepare rosters, launch games, track live game activity and rotations, and review playing time and player statistics after the game.

## Positioning

BenchMaster centers a fast live coaching workflow: coaches can manage rotations and playing time during a game, then use the recorded timeline and usage summary to understand how players were used.

## Operating Context

The app is mobile-first and supports both guest use on the current browser/device and signed-in use with data persisted through the service. Coaches create teams and rosters, select available players and starters for a game, manage the live game and its periods, then review a summary. The interface is available in French and English.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Guest mode stores one team and one game locally in the browser.
- Signed-in mode stores teams and games in the backend and scopes them to the account.
- Coaches can manage teams and players; prepare games and starting lineups; start, pause, resume, and end games; manage periods and substitutions; record and correct player statistics; and review playing time, starter/bench split, usage insights, and the rotation timeline.
- The product is basketball-specific. Preserve its basketball terminology and coaching workflows.
- The user confirmed the fast live coaching workflow as the product's core distinction.
